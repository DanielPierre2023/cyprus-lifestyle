# Cyprus Lifestyle — Editorial & Product Concept

*The House Book, Volume I*

## The idea
Cyprus sits where **Europe, the Levant and the Gulf** meet, and no publication
owns that crossroads. Cyprus Lifestyle is the record of how the island lives,
invests and enjoys itself at the top end — *the island, elevated*. Glamorous like
the fashion books (Vogue, Harper's Bazaar), authoritative like the living titles
(Condé Nast Traveller, Robb Report, Architectural Digest), and **multilingual by
birth**.

- **Primary tagline:** The Island, Elevated.
- **Secondary:** Kýpros, in a finer light.
- **Voice:** assured, not loud · worldly, not distant · warm, not casual · precise, never fussy.
- **Audience:** international investors & relocators; the Cypriot elite at home; the Gulf's visitors & capital; the Romanian professional community. Affluent, worldly, 30–65.

| 6 | **The Business of the Island** | Economy, tech & shipping, tax & residency | *The Ledger* — one number that matters |

## Signature franchises
- **The Island Index** *(annual)* — the definitive "Best of Cyprus". The issue everyone keeps.
- **The Dispatch** *(weekly email)* — property, money and culture, every Friday.
- **The Circle** *(membership)* — events, print edition, directory, concierge.
- **The Cover Story** *(monthly)* — one long, photographed feature, in all four languages.
- **At Home With…** *(fortnightly)* — inside a significant Cypriot residence.
- **The Reservation** *(rolling)* — a living map of where to eat, stay and be seen.

## Cadence
Daily web-first stories · **weekly** Dispatch · **quarterly** print edition ·
**annual** Island Index.

One canonical article → many `article_translations` rows; `dir` and locale per
translation; `hreflang` + localized slugs for SEO. The AI translator drafts; a
human editor approves before publish.

## Principles for the newsroom
- AI **assists**, humans **decide**. Nothing publishes without editorial sign-off.
- Every claim the AI can't verify becomes a **fact-flag** for the desk.
- Every model call is logged (cost, tokens, latency) for **observability**.
- Restraint reads as expensive; specifics read as true.
